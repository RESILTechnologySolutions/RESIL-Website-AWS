The firm

RESIL Technology Solutions

RESIL Technology Solutions LLC is an SBA-certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business headquartered in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, established in April 2026. We focus on cloud infrastructure, DevOps support, secure system configuration, and IT consulting for government and commercial clients.

As a founder-led firm pursuing federal subcontracting work, every engagement gets direct, hands-on attention from start to finish. We partner with prime contractors across the Milwaukee metro and Wisconsin to deliver technical capability where it is needed most.

RESIL combines computer science fundamentals with hands-on experience across AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, GitHub Actions, and Linux, backed by disciplined documentation practices and an active certification pathway.

We hold registered NAICS codes covering computer systems design, custom software development, computer facilities management, cloud infrastructure and hosting, and computer training.
